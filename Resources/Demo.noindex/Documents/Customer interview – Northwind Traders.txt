CUSTOMER INTERVIEW – NORTHWIND TRADERS
Contoso Outdoor · product team · Lucía Romero · Tuesday, October 13, 2026


WHO AND WHEN

Raj Mehta, head of buying at Northwind Traders: outdoor and travel gear, 24
stores in France, an online shop and a warehouse in Lyon. They have sold the
Trailhead 2 since 2024. He has seen the Trailhead 3 under NDA and knows that
nothing about it is public before Tuesday, November 3, at 9:00.

Call on Tuesday, October 13, from 12:00 to 12:45. Raj phoned Emma, in key
accounts, to place an order, and Emma recorded that part with his OK: "Call –
Northwind Traders, Trailhead 3 order", in Audio. Then she brought me in for
the half-hour interview I had asked her for.


THE ORDER, IN SHORT

  - 300 Trailhead 3 for their spring catalog, at 249 € list with the 12 %
    discount for orders over 250 units: 74,700 € list, 65,736 € after the
    discount.
  - Two deliveries to the warehouse in Lyon, same discount for both: 150 tents
    in the first week of March and 150 in the first week of April.
  - Emma sends the quote today. Raj gets the purchase order signed this week.
  - Colors: he'll split the 300 in the purchase order. Sand sells better in
    their stores in the south, forest green in the north.


WHAT THEIR CUSTOMERS ASK ABOUT A TENT

  - Weight, first. "Under two kilos is the line people look at." At 1.9 kg the
    Trailhead 3 is under it; at 2.2 kg the Trailhead 2 never was.
  - Pitching it alone, in the wind or in the dark. His staff will pitch one in
    the shop to show the color-coded poles: if it goes up in three minutes in
    front of a customer, it sells itself.
  - Room for three with their bags: the two doors and two vestibules answer it
    before people ask.
  - What happens if a pole snaps on a trip. The repair sleeve in the bag and
    five years of warranty on the poles (two on the rest) are the answer he
    wants printed in the catalog.
  - The recycled fly matters to younger customers, but nobody buys a tent for
    the fabric alone.


RETURNS: WHERE IT HURTS

Northwind takes back what their customers return in store. When a Contoso
product is faulty, it comes back to us through a wholesale return by email: a
form, photos, an RMA number and a courier they book themselves. Raj: "Two
weeks of emails for one broken pole is too long."

He asked whether his store staff could use our returns assistant, the way our
own customers do since September: an RMA number and a courier pickup in one
chat. Not today. From November 2 the pilot opens to our own sales team, not to
partners, and an outside company would need its own sign-in and a check that
every order it asks about is its own (CX-149). I said I'd take it to the 2027
planning, and that's all I promised.


WHAT WE CAN AND CAN'T SAY

Raj wanted "the lightest three-person tent on the market" for the catalog. No:
we can't prove it, and legal has asked us never to say it. What we can say:
1.9 kg, 300 g lighter than the Trailhead 2, up in three minutes. Marta sends
Northwind the press kit and the product photos on October 28, under the same
embargo, and reads their catalog copy before it goes to print
(prensa@contoso.com).
