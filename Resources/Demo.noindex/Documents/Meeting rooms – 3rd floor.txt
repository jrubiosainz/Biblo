Book a room in Outlook: add it to the meeting the way you add a person. The
call booths can't be booked; if someone is waiting, 30 minutes at most.

Ártico is the only room with a camera, so hybrid meetings and workshops go
first there. A booking nobody turns up to within 15 minutes is released for
others.


THE KITCHEN

Coffee, water and a microwave, at the east end of the floor, past the open
space. The newcomers' coffee is here on Fridays at 10:00. Please leave cups in
the dishwasher, not in the sink.

THE IT DESK

Megan is at the IT desk, between the kitchen and the call booths, from 9:00 to
18:00: a new laptop, a broken charger, a password that doesn't work, a strange
email. Any other time, call extension 3300, which answers 24 hours a day, or
write to it@contoso.com. If you think you clicked a phishing link, call
straight away.
