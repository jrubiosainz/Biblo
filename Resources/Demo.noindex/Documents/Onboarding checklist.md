# Onboarding checklist – your first month at Contoso Outdoor

*People & Culture · Madrid office · updated October 9, 2026 · the same steps as the welcome video, "Bienvenida a Contoso"*

Welcome! This is everything you need for your first month, in the order you'll need it. Tick things off as you go, and ask your buddy about anything that isn't here.

## Day 1

- [ ] **8:30–10:00 · Pick up your access card** at reception, on the ground floor. Bring your DNI or passport.
- [ ] **10:00 · Get your laptop** at the IT desk on the 3rd floor, next to the kitchen. Megan is there from 9:00 to 18:00 and sets it up with you: sign-in, Microsoft Authenticator, Teams and Outlook.
- [ ] Say hello on Teams in **#bienvenida**: who you are, which team you've joined, and one thing you like doing outdoors.
- [ ] Lunch with your manager. It's on us.

## Week 1

- [ ] **The security course**, 30 minutes on the training platform, before Friday. It shows you how to spot a phishing email, and what to do if you click one anyway.
- [ ] Read the travel and expenses policy (*Política de viajes y gastos*) before you book any trip.
- [ ] Book your meeting rooms from Outlook: there's a guide to the rooms on the 3rd floor in *Meeting rooms – 3rd floor*.
- [ ] Ask your manager for access to your team's SharePoint site and Teams channels.

## Month 1

- [ ] **Meet your buddy**: someone from your team, for all the questions that aren't in any handbook. Your manager introduces you on day 1.
- [ ] **Friday 10:00 · Newcomers' coffee** in the kitchen on the 3rd floor, every Friday. Come to as many as you like.
- [ ] Set your goals for the first three months with your manager.
- [ ] Look at the 2027 holiday calendar (*Calendario laboral 2027*) before you plan your holidays.
